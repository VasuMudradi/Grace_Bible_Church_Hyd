Verse of the day

Welcome home

A family of believers, saved by grace and sent in love

Grace Bible Church is a local congregation in RC Puram where the Bible is open, prayer is constant and every person is known by name. Whether you have walked with the Lord for decades or are just beginning, there is a seat for you.

The Word

We teach the Bible book by book and live under its authority.

Grace

We are saved by grace through faith, and we extend that same grace to others.

Prayer

Every week begins and ends on our knees, together and in our homes.

Fellowship

Men, women, youth and children grow side by side as one family.

Mission

We give, pray and go so that villages and cities hear the gospel.

    Men's and Women's meetings

    Brothers and sisters, growing together

    Men's Meeting

    Iron sharpens iron

    Men of all ages gather for the Word, honest conversation and prayer for their homes, work and witness.

    • Now reading: The Measure of a Man by Gene A. Getz
    • Bible study and testimony
    • Prayer for families and workplaces
    • Serving the church and neighbourhood
    Every 1st and 3rd Wednesday, 6:00 PM at the church hall

    Women's Fellowship

    Strength and dignity

    Women meet to study Scripture, encourage one another and pray for the church, children and the sick.

    • Bible study and worship in song
    • Visiting the sick and the lonely
    • Mothers' prayer for the next generation
    Second Saturday of every month, 4:00 PM at the church hall

    Missionary work

    Go and make disciples

    "Go ye therefore, and teach all nations." Matthew 28:19. Our church supports the gospel beyond our walls through prayer, giving and going.

    Village outreach

    Teams travel to nearby villages to share the gospel, pray with families and hold children's programmes.

    Bibles and literature

    Scriptures and gospel tracts are placed in homes and shared in the languages people read.

    Care and compassion

    Food, medicine and encouragement for families in need, shown in the name of Christ.

    Missionaries we support

    We stand with missionaries and church planters through monthly giving, letters and prayer.

    Pray for the mission field

    • Open doors and open hearts in every village we visit
    • Safety and strength for our missionaries and their families
    • Workers for the harvest from our own congregation
    • New believers to be discipled and rooted in the Word

    Missionary outreach meets on the third Sunday of each month at 2:00 PM, after worship.
