About Us

For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith—and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God—not by works, so that no one can boast.Ephesians 2:8-9

Grace Bible Church is a local congregation in RC Puram, Hyderabad. We teach the Bible, we pray together, and we hold out the gospel of grace to our neighbours and to the nations.

Our Mission

Why we exist: to know Christ, grow in His Word and carry His grace to every home in our city and to the nations.
